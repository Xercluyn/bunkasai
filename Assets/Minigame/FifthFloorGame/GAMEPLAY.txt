(START)

	- The opening scene begins with the arrival of an aircraft, from which an agent jumps out from the 	  rear.
	- The second scene starts with an angle facing the landing spot (where the agent arrives in the 	  gameplay area), followed by an on-screen explanation of the gameplay.

(GAMEPLAY)

	- Gameplay begins with an instructional segment (a tutorial featuring images). There are three types of 	  apartment layouts:

		* Green Doors: These are the locations where the player must deliver a newspaper (triggering 		  the rhythm game sequence). If the player passes by one of these doors without delivering a 		  newspaper, will it result in a score deduction, or simply count as a "miss"?
		
		* Red Doors: These are traps. They feature a mechanism similar to the green doors, but with a 		  catch: delivering a newspaper here incurs a penalty. Therefore, the player must bypass or 		  ignore them.
		
		* Yellow Doors: These represent a standard layout (no events occur here).

(ENDING)

	- The ending is reached when the player arrives at the extraction zone and activates the trigger (the 	  helipad). The camera angle then shifts to a low-angle view, showing the aircraft departing.
